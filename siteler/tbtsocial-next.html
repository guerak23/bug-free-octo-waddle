Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI platform work?

Our engine connects real-time trend feeds to the Claude API. It drafts brand-aligned scripts, triggers ElevenLabs for AI voiceovers, and syncs approval cards to Trello before publishing via Make.com.

Can I connect our custom agency tech stack via API?

Yes. tbtsocial provides full REST endpoints, webhook triggers, and 1,500+ pre-built Make.com integrations so you can seamlessly link your CRM, Slack, and publishing channels.

What are the SaaS retainer and subscription models?

We provide flexible monthly SaaS retainers scaled to your monthly pipeline volume. From boutique agencies managing 5 pipelines to enterprise teams running autonomous multi-brand operations.
